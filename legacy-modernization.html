Legacy Modernization

Status: Production_Released

Objective_Summary

Modernized a complex legacy computer-aided engineering (CAE) application for Mitsubishi Fuso, Japan. The system was originally built using Perl CGI and Shell scripts, making it difficult to maintain and scale.

I managed the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) independently—from reverse engineering the legacy codebase to architecture design, development, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and final production release.

  • check_circle
    Stakeholder Alignment Collaborated directly with Japanese stakeholders to ensure requirement parity and timely delivery.
